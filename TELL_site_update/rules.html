Rules

TELL is a free Python trading-bot competition where developers write bots and compete in a fictional market.

These rules apply to Season 1. We may clarify them before the season starts and will note every change here.

1. Entry

  • Entry is free. There is no fee to register, submit, or win.
  • All trading uses play money. No real money is traded, and play money cannot be exchanged for anything.
  • One account per person. Teams of one are the default; team rules will be announced with the season date.
  • Anyone can play. Prizes are only for players 18 or older who can verify their identity.

2. Season 1 timing

  • We announce the start date once pre-registrations reach 40. The season starts about three weeks after the announcement.
  • If we don't reach 40, we still run a no-prize Season 0.

3. Prizes

  • The $20 prize pool unlocks when Season 1 has 40 valid players: $12 for first place and $8 for second.
  • A valid player submits at least one bot that runs to the end without errors before the deadline.
  • Part of the site's future ad revenue is added to the next season's prize. The added amount is published on the site.
  • Prizes are paid in cash or as a digital gift card suited to the winner's country. Eligible countries are announced with the season date.
  • Players are responsible for any taxes on prizes where they live.

4. Your bot

  • A bot is a Python class with an on_tick(state) method that returns a list of orders. The practice kit includes a template.
  • You may build your bot any way you like, including with AI tools or models you trained yourself.
  • Your bot only sees the current and past state of the market. It never sees future prices.
  • Each product has a position limit. Orders beyond it are cut automatically.

5. Scoring

  • Each scenario is 2,000 ticks. A bot's score in a scenario is its profit and loss, with open positions marked at the final mid price.
  • During the season, a public leaderboard scores submissions on practice scenarios.
  • Final rankings use the average profit across 500 hidden scenarios. These scenarios stay private until the season ends.
  • Before the season starts, we publish a fingerprint (a hash) of the hidden scenario list. After the season, we publish the list itself, so anyone can check it was not changed.
  • Rankings show a margin of error. Bots whose results are within that margin are shown as tied.

6. How submissions run

  • Season submissions run in an isolated environment with no internet access, a time limit per tick, and a memory limit.
  • In the practice market, your bot runs inside your own browser. Your code is not sent to us.
  • The number of submissions per day may be limited, so that no one can tune a bot to the hidden scenarios.

7. Fair play

  • The organizer's own bots may appear on the leaderboard for reference but are never eligible for prizes.
  • Hidden market details, including how each product is generated, are never published.
  • Trying to read hidden data, attack the platform, or use more than one account leads to disqualification.
  • Before prizes are paid, we review the code of winning bots and re-run them to confirm the result.

8. Sponsors

  • Sponsors may provide prizes, credits or services and are credited on the site.
  • Sponsors have no say in rules, scoring or rankings, and get no access to hidden scenarios or to players' code.

9. Your data

  • We use your email only for TELL updates. We don't sell or share it.
  • Your nickname appears on leaderboards. Your code is not published without your permission.

10. Questions

Email tell.tradingbot@gmail.com.

TELL is an independently run play-money simulation competition. Nothing here is investment advice.
