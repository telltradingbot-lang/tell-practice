TELL is a free Python trading-bot competition where developers write bots and compete in a fictional market.

TELL is currently in pre-registration, with a working public practice environment already available.

How it works

1

Write a bot

A Python class with one method, on_tick(state), that returns buy and sell orders. A template is included.

2

Practice in your browser

Upload your bot to the practice market. It runs inside your browser on 50 public scenarios and replays every trade.

3

Compete on hidden scenarios

Season rankings use average profit across 500 scenarios nobody has seen, so tuning to the practice set won't win.

The market

Four fictional products trade in a fictional city. Each one moves differently, and each one hides something. Bots that trade at random lose. Bots that find the structure win.

4fictional products
2,000ticks per scenario
50public practice scenarios
500hidden scenarios for rankings

Season 1

Free to enter

No entry fee. Trading uses play money only, and nothing can be cashed out.

$20 prize at 40 players

The prize unlocks once 40 valid players enter: $12 for first, $8 for second. Part of future ad revenue is added on top.

Date set at 40 sign-ups

We announce the start date when pre-registrations reach 40. If we don't get there, we still run a no-prize Season 0.

Fair play

  • Free to enter
  • No real money is traded
  • The organizer's own bots are not eligible for prizes
  • Final ranking scenarios stay private until the season ends
  • Season submissions run in an isolated environment with no internet access
  • Prizes are for players 18 or older
  • Sponsors have no say in rankings
  • Hidden market details are never published

Roadmap

  1. PHASE 1

    Practice environment and browser playDone

    Market generator, match engine, 50 practice scenarios, an open-source practice kit, and a page that runs bots in the browser.

  2. PHASE 2

    Pre-registration and communityNow

    Gathering the first 40 players and early feedback on the market design.

  3. PHASE 3

    Secure execution for submitted bots

    Sandboxed runs with no internet access and time limits, scoring on hidden scenarios.

  4. PHASE 4

    Season 1

    The first official season with a public leaderboard and prizes.

  5. PHASE 5

    Rankings, replays and sponsors

    Persistent rankings, shareable replays, titles, and sponsor-backed prizes.
