TELL sponsor brief

TELL is a free Python trading-bot competition where developers write bots and compete in a fictional market.

TELL is currently in pre-registration, with a working public practice environment already available. October 2026.

What is already built

  • A browser practice market. Players upload a Python bot and it runs inside their browser on 50 practice scenarios, with an animated replay of every trade. No install.
  • An open-source practice kit on GitHub (MIT license): match engine, practice data, bot template, examples and a Colab notebook.
  • A tested market generator. Across 200 test scenarios, random and simple strategies lose money, while a bot that understands the market's structure wins in 92% of scenarios. Results depend on skill, not luck.
  • Public rules, pre-registration and a launch trailer.

Who is building it

TELL is built and run by a university student at San Diego State University, who designed the market, wrote the engine and the browser version, and runs the project. Bilingual in English and Japanese.

Why it is interesting

  • Skill over luck. Each product hides a different pattern. Winning means finding it, not guessing direction.
  • Built for the AI era. Players can design bots with AI tools, train their own models, or write everything by hand. The market rewards understanding either way.
  • Always open. Most trading competitions run for a couple of weeks a year. TELL's practice market is available all year.
  • Two languages. The site and materials are in English and Japanese, reaching both developer communities.
